Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11th, 2026 · Placeholder wording — replace with MXN's own legal text before launch.

MXN ("MXN", "we", "us" or "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, share and protect personal information when you visit our website, buy from us or contact us.

It applies when you:

  • Visit the MXN website
  • Create an account or join the MXN List
  • Buy, sell or trade with us
  • Request a bespoke design
  • Contact our concierge by chat, phone, text or email
  • Attend a private viewing or MXN event

1. Information We Collect

We collect the details you give us when you browse, join the MXN List, place an order or contact us, the information collected automatically when you use our website, and information from trusted partners.

Information you give us

  • Contact and account information: name, email address, phone number, delivery and billing addresses, and the password you choose if you create an account.
  • Purchase information: the pieces you buy, sizes, metal choices, engravings, gift messages, delivery instructions, returns and exchanges.
  • Payment information: card details are collected and processed by our payment providers; MXN only receives the card type, last four digits and expiry date.
  • Bespoke design information: sketches, photographs, reference pictures, measurements and notes you share with our designers for a custom piece.
  • Communications: messages, emails, texts and calls with our concierge, including any reviews or feedback you send.
  • Marketing preferences: your email address or phone number if you join the MXN List, and the topics you are interested in.

Information collected automatically

  • Device and usage information: IP address, browser and device type, operating system, pages and products viewed, search terms, referring sites, time on site and approximate location based on your IP address.
  • Email and text engagement: whether you open our emails or texts and which links you select.
  • Cookies and similar technologies: as described in section 6.

Information from other sources

  • Service providers such as payment processors, fraud-prevention partners and delivery carriers.
  • Social media platforms, when you interact with MXN on them or with our advertisements.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use personal information to:

  • Process, verify, make and deliver your orders
  • Create and service the bespoke pieces you commission
  • Answer questions and provide aftercare, cleaning, resizing and repairs
  • Manage your account, wish list and order history
  • Send news about MXN when you have agreed to it
  • Personalise your experience, including recommendations based on pieces you have viewed
  • Measure and improve our website, products and service
  • Prevent fraud and keep our website, customers and staff secure
  • Comply with legal obligations, respond to lawful requests and enforce our terms

3. How We Disclose Your Information

We disclose personal information only to:

  • Commerce and technology providers that host our website, run our shop, process payments and send emails and texts
  • Shipping and insurance providers that deliver and insure your pieces
  • Fraud-prevention partners that help us screen high-value orders
  • Professional advisers such as lawyers, accountants and auditors
  • Authorities when required by law or to protect our rights and customers
  • A successor business in connection with a merger, acquisition or sale of assets
  • Others, with your consent

4. Fraud Prevention and Verification

Because we sell high-value fine jewellery, we use automated fraud screening and manual review. If an order requires verification, we may ask for a government ID, a card verification, proof of address or a short video call. If verification is not completed the order may be delayed or cancelled. We use verification information only for security, fraud prevention, legal compliance and dispute resolution.

5. Email and Text Message Marketing

If you subscribe, we may email you about new arrivals, private previews, events and our launch. Every marketing email includes an unsubscribe link. If you opt in to text messages:

  • Consent is not a condition of any purchase
  • Message frequency varies; message and data rates may apply
  • Reply STOP to cancel or HELP for help at any time
  • Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages
  • We will still send service messages about your orders, deliveries and appointments

6. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We use the following kinds of cookies and similar technologies:

  • Required: needed to sign in, keep items in your bag and check out; these cannot be switched off
  • Preferences: remember your settings, currency and recently viewed pieces
  • Analytics: help us understand how visitors use the site
  • Advertising: used by us and our advertising partners to show relevant MXN news on other sites

7. Data Retention

We keep personal information only as long as needed for the purposes in this policy:

  • Order, warranty and authenticity records: for as long as the piece is covered by our guarantee, and as long as the law requires for tax and accounting
  • Identity verification materials: only as long as needed to complete verification, resolve any dispute and meet legal obligations
  • Bespoke design files: for as long as needed to make, service and reorder your piece, unless you ask us to delete them
  • Marketing data: until you unsubscribe, plus a suppression record so we do not contact you again

8. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • Know what personal information we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Delete your personal information
  • Receive a portable copy of your information
  • Object to, or ask us to limit, how we use it
  • Withdraw consent to marketing at any time
  • Complain to your local data protection authority

9. Data Security

We use administrative, technical and physical safeguards to protect your information, including encryption, restricted access and confidentiality agreements with our staff and providers. No system is completely secure, so please contact us at once if you believe your account has been compromised.

10. Children

Our website is intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Third-Party Sites and Services

Our site may link to third-party sites, including payment providers, delivery carriers and social media platforms. Their own privacy policies govern how they handle your information.

12. International Visitors

MXN is based in Ireland. If you visit from outside the European Economic Area, your information will be processed in Ireland and in other countries where our service providers operate, using appropriate safeguards such as standard contractual clauses.

13. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed, and we will tell you about important changes by email or with a notice on the site.
